What is
Obstetrics &
Gynecology

OB/GYN is a medical specialty that focuses on women's reproductive health, pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum care. Obstetrics deals with pregnancy and postpartum care, while gynecology deals with the female reproductive system outside of pregnancy. OB/GYNs are medical doctors who are trained in both obstetrics & gynecology, and they provide healthcare services to women throughout their reproductive lives.
